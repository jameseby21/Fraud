# Analytical review memo

## Question and input

Course/lesson, synthetic flag, dataset version/hash, row grain, source limitations, tool/version.

## Reproduce

Commands or query, filters, units, grouping keys, dates, row count, and output location.

## Observations

Transaction IDs and computed quantities. Distinguish what the data records from what actually happened.

## Hypotheses and counterevidence

Possible mechanism, at least one benign alternative, false positives, missing evidence, and uncertainty.

## Next evidence and review

What authorized/public record would discriminate alternatives? What result would change your interpretation? Reviewer/date and corrections.

## Learning assessment

Predictions before analysis, hints/key used, missed patterns, incidental matches, and next practice task. Fixture success is not a real-world accuracy estimate.
