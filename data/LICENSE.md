# Data reuse

Original project-authored factual metadata in `cases.json`, `case_outcomes.json`, and `evidence.jsonl`, and synthetic observations in `synthetic_replays.jsonl`, are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). To the extent contributors hold copyright or related rights in those original contributions, they waive them under that instrument. Facts about historical events are not claimed as property.

External government documents and API responses are not relicensed here. Their original status and terms govern. Retain attribution, provenance, and notices. The project grants no rights to protected seals, third-party photos, exhibits, or embedded assets. Do not reuse source-site branding as project branding.

The real baseball source CSV and derived baseball-real-v1 summaries retain CC BY 4.0: Nathan David Pifer (2024), Optical Tracking Data from College Baseball Scrimmages, Mendeley Data V3, doi:10.17632/xfnz6mkdzm.3. See [the source guide](baseball/real-v1/README.md) for the license link and changes. The original CSV is unchanged, summary calculations are documented, and no endorsement is implied. This source is not relicensed as project CC0. The separate project-authored synthetic baseball-v1 CSV/answer key are dedicated under CC0-1.0, alongside the original synthetic materials described above.

The USAspending CSV normalizes public federal spending facts from the retained API response. The CFTC reference CSV normalizes 15 public aggregate open-interest facts from a retained government API response. Their provenance and field interpretations should travel with them. Inclusion implies neither misconduct nor government endorsement. See `../DATA_SOURCES.md`.
