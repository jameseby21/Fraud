# Data reuse

Original project-authored factual metadata in `cases.json` and `evidence.jsonl`, and synthetic observations in `synthetic_replays.jsonl`, are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). To the extent contributors hold copyright or related rights in those original contributions, they waive them under that instrument. Facts about historical events are not claimed as property.

External government documents and API responses are not relicensed here. Their original status and terms govern. Retain attribution, provenance, and notices. The project grants no rights to protected seals, third-party photos, exhibits, or embedded assets. Do not reuse source-site branding as project branding.

The USAspending CSV normalizes public federal spending facts from the retained API response. Its provenance and field interpretation should travel with it. Inclusion implies neither misconduct nor government endorsement. See `../DATA_SOURCES.md`.
