Criterion: amount_cents modulo 100 equals zero; optionally inspect selected round multiples. Eight planted USD 2,500 rows blend with copied real values, many of which are also round. Compare counts and proportions within category/supplier/time, show exact rows and identify benign pricing/fee conventions. Round figures can be normal. They become useful only with corroborating documentary or behavioral discrepancies, and are not proof of fraud.
