# Earlier introductory patterns (payments-v1)

The original 6,000-row dataset remains a stable beginner exercise. It uses bounded transformed award magnitudes and obvious introductory targets. It is separate from the richer exact-value forensic-v2 case.

- Threshold grouping: three below-threshold USD 9,900 payments total USD 29,700; the full rule returns five groups, four incidental.
- Amount outlier: one USD 275,000 transaction is outside the bounded background; a capital purchase is a benign alternative.
- Timing: one 181-day delay; disputes, recording errors and processing delays are alternatives.
- Recurring service control: equal amounts on distinct monthly invoices are not duplicate invoices.

See the version-1 lessons and instructor key for exact IDs and arithmetic. None of the thresholds is asserted as a real legal rule. Fixture success does not establish performance on real data.
