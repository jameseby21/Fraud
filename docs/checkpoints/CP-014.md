# CP-014: diverse resolved-case outcomes

Acceptance tests were written first and failed on the old 20-case catalog, missing structured resolutions, absent acquittal event type, missing generated guides, and absent UI control. The implementation adds six primary-source case records and a separate `case_outcomes.json` layer, bringing the catalog to 26 resolved records plus five unresolved research priorities.

The additions cover a CFPB administrative consent order, two deferred prosecution agreements, a corporate guilty plea and parent-company deferred resolution, a criminal acquittal, and a separate SEC civil final judgment. The HealthSouth acquittal remains actor- and proceeding-specific; other participants' pleas do not change it. The criminal and civil Scrushy proceedings have separate IDs and sources. Existing mixed verdicts and a partial reversal are also explicit.

The UI can filter resolved records by criminal disposition and displays forum, resolution type, imprisonment when sourced in the catalog, and monetary remedies with qualifiers and scope. Restitution, civil penalty, forfeiture, disgorgement, coordinated payments, and loss are not collapsed into a severity score or a shared damages field. Outcome metadata is excluded from the inputs and target labels of every synthetic mechanism replay.

The append-only intake registered the six new official records. Automated retrieval retained the CFPB consent order, GovInfo court document, and SEC release. Three DOJ pages were available for review but rejected by the machine evidence checks; both restricted-network failures and unrestricted attempts remain in the log, and no artifact was fabricated. Generated guides, documentation, and source limits were updated with the catalog.

Focused validation passed 45 casebook, HTTP, learning, and record-library tests. Headless Edge passed 26-record counts, acquittal filtering and display, case search, source-date gating, synthetic replay, unresolved-case separation, public-data quality, course behavior, and mobile layout. The first union run found a stale fixed-count test expecting the original 25 evidence rows and 60 synthetic observations; its intended counts were updated to 31 and 78. Final verification passed 138 tests with zero skips, 84 staged-data byte checks, 88 linked documents, 31 retained source revisions, all fixture and generated-material checks, publication scanning, and syntax checks. Power BI Desktop and interactive local R acceptance remain manual.
